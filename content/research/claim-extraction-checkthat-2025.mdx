We compared fine-tuning and prompting setups for claim extraction. FLAN-T5 won on METEOR, while iterative self-refinement sometimes produced claims a fact-checker would actually want to work with. That gap is the whole plot: evaluation has to stay connected to the user and the work the system is meant to support.
