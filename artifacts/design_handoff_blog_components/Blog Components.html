[ 01 ]

Cover / banner image

Full-width banner at the very top of the page, above the header-adjacent title block — 21:9, cropped, one hairline border, no caption. This is the one place the system allows a photograph; for data-heavy pieces, prefer an instrument panel (score/trace) instead, per the design system's imagery rule.

[ 11 ]

Content width & container

Article shell: 200px TOC rail + up to 940px content column inside the 1224px page max. Paragraphs and headings stay at the 730px prose measure; code, figures, tables and embeds are allowed to use the full content column as a breakout.
