## D7 content taxonomy

Fern uses one MDX pipeline and a `type`-like split (blog vs case study) rather than a `/blog` URL implying one genre. We do the same under `/writing/[slug]`.

### Long-form (one pipeline)

All essays, logs, explainers, and course notes are writing.

| `type` | Folder | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `case-study` | `content/writing/case-studies/` | Finished project or research system. Problem → approach → result. Standalone engineering posts land here; there is no `blog` / `eng-blog` type. |
| `build-log` | `content/writing/build-logs/` | Numbered entries in a series (`series` + `part`). |
| `research` | `content/writing/research/` | Explainer or opinion tied to a paper. Not the publication record itself. |
| `teaching` | `content/writing/teaching/` | Course material while TAing. |

Shared Zod fields (`lib/schemas.ts` `writingFrontmatterSchema`): `title`, `date` (publication day `YYYY-MM-DD`, not a timestamp), `description`, `type`, `tags`, `draft`. Optional: `series`, `part`, `paper` (DOI or URL), `course` (e.g. `CS 6xx · Fall 2026`), `cover` (relative path under `content/assets/`). Git history is created/updated. No `updated` field — git is enough; the index does not show “updated”.

Index: `/writing` with filters by `type`. Series pages can group `build-log` parts. Fern adoption is **not** a public `build-log` series.

Voice: one `voice-nikhil.md` plus short overlays per `type` (narrative for case studies, citation-heavy for research, example-heavy for teaching).

### Catalogs (not the writing pipeline)

| Surface | URL | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Projects | `/projects`, `/projects/[slug]` | Shipped work cards. Links, status, repo. A case-study post may point here. |
| Research | `/research`, `/research/[slug]` | Publication record: year, venue, PDF, arXiv. A `type: research` post may point here. |

Do not flatten papers and repos into `/writing`. Do not create `/research/[slug]` MDX essays that duplicate `type: research` posts.

### Seed content

- `content/writing/research/the-score-is-not-the-work.mdx` — `type: research`, `description`, live at `/writing/the-score-is-not-the-work`
- Fern wave notes: `artifacts/fern-template-adoption/CHANGELOG.md` (metrics) and `artifacts/fern-template-adoption/notes/` (prose). Not under `content/writing/`.
- `content/research/claim-extraction-checkthat-2025.mdx` → still a catalog seed; the live catalog record is `lib/content.ts`. Optional writing explainer is the research post above.
- `content/projects/*.mdx` → project catalog records, not case-study posts until we write real case studies
